Features

A front door that answers questions.

Rails and themed playlists hope a physician finds the right film. A question bar finds the right moment, and tells you what people wanted to know.

A walled garden

Only your films, their transcripts and the slides shown in them. The AI reads every slide too. Nothing from the outside world gets in.

Ask any question

One simple search bar with suggested questions. It searches every transcript across the whole library, not one film at a time.

Watch the answer

Press play and the relevant clips run back to back, with a short summary that lists every source film and timecode.

Stays on label

No rephrasing, no recutting, no new claims. If the answer isn't in your approved content, it says so and doesn't answer.

Quick self-checks

After a few questions, physicians can take a short A, B, C or D quiz built from the same material, then watch the answer to each one.

See what they want to know

An admin dashboard shows who logged on, what they asked, which questions the library answered and where the gaps are.

FAQ

Questions from medical affairs.

Does the AI write new medical content?

No. It searches your approved films and serves the exact moments that answer the question. AI is used to search and to help cut clips, never to create claims.

What happens if someone asks something outside our content?

It doesn't answer. The library is a walled garden, so a question your films don't cover gets no answer, and it shows up on your dashboard as a gap.

Can marketing download clips straight away?

No. A new clip sits in the medical affairs review queue and can't be downloaded until it's approved.

Does AI triage replace our approval system?

No. Triage is a fast first pass against the documents you upload. Final approval still goes through your own system.

Is a short clip from an approved film easy to approve?

Usually. The source is already approved, so a short cut that keeps the required disclaimers is a much lighter review than new content.
